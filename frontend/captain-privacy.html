Captain Privacy Policy

Your information matters. Learn how ShuttleConnect intends to collect, use, protect and manage your personal information when you apply to become a Captain.

Important notice: This is a draft privacy policy for the ShuttleConnect pilot. It describes intended data practices and must be reviewed against the actual application, backend, hosting, storage and payment systems before launch. It is not a statement that all listed safeguards or services are already implemented.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to individuals who apply for, register for or use the ShuttleConnect Captain platform.

It explains the categories of information that may be processed during application, verification, account usage and ride operations.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ShuttleConnect may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal details: Full name, email address, mobile number and residential address.
  • Vehicle information: Vehicle category, registration number, make, model and manufacturing year.
  • Driving licence information: Licence number, validity and expiry date.
  • Verification documents: Driving licence, vehicle registration certificate, insurance and other documents required for verification.
  • Account information: Login identifiers, account status and security-related records.
  • Ride information: Ride requests, assigned rides, pickup and destination details, ride status and cancellation records.
  • Financial information: Ride fares, commission calculations, payment status and settlement records.
  • Technical information: Device, browser, application activity and security logs, where collected.
  • Location information: Location data where a location-dependent feature is enabled and the required permissions and notices are provided.

3. How We May Use Your Information

Your information may be used for legitimate platform-related purposes, including:

  • Processing and reviewing Captain applications.
  • Verifying identity, driving eligibility and vehicle documents.
  • Creating, activating and maintaining Captain accounts.
  • Providing authentication and account-recovery services.
  • Sending ride requests and communicating assignment details.
  • Managing ride status, passenger coordination and support.
  • Calculating eligible fares, platform commission and Captain earnings.
  • Processing payment and settlement records.
  • Investigating complaints, suspected fraud and safety incidents.
  • Maintaining platform security and improving service reliability.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Document Verification

Documents submitted during the Captain application process may be reviewed by authorized personnel for eligibility and verification purposes.

  • Documents should be used only for the stated verification purposes and related legal obligations.
  • Access should be restricted to authorized personnel who need the information for their work.
  • Documents should not be publicly accessible through application URLs.
  • Retention and deletion should follow a documented policy and applicable legal requirements.
Document upload and secure storage must be implemented on the backend before real applications are accepted. Selecting a file in the current frontend demonstration does not upload or save that document.

5. Location Information

If ShuttleConnect introduces location-based ride features, it may process location information necessary to support those features.

  • Location access should be requested only when required by an enabled feature.
  • The purpose and relevant permissions should be explained to Captains.
  • Location data should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and notice.
  • Location retention and access should be limited according to the platform's final data policy.

6. When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared where necessary for operating the platform, fulfilling rides or meeting legal obligations.

  • Passengers: Relevant Captain details may be shared to identify the assigned Captain and coordinate a confirmed ride.
  • Administrators: Authorized administrators may access application, verification, ride and support information for operational purposes.
  • Service providers: Hosting, email, payment, mapping and other providers may process relevant information when required for their services.
  • Authorities: Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Information should not be sold to third parties as a standalone commercial data product.

7. Account Security

ShuttleConnect intends to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect account and application information.

  • Passwords should be stored using secure password hashing rather than plaintext.
  • Account access should be restricted through role-based authorization.
  • Password activation and reset links should be single-use and time-limited.
  • Access to verification documents should be restricted.
  • Security incidents should be investigated and handled under an established response process.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Captains should protect their login credentials and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

8. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including applicable legal, accounting, safety and dispute-resolution requirements.

The final retention schedule should specify how long application records, verification documents, ride records, financial records and security logs are retained, and how they are securely deleted or anonymized when no longer required.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and verification requirements, Captains may be able to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where the information is no longer required and deletion is legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
  • Raise concerns about the use or handling of personal information.

Requests should be handled through the official privacy contact channel once it has been established.

10. Third-Party Services

ShuttleConnect may rely on third-party providers for services such as hosting, email delivery, maps, routing and payment processing.

Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. The platform should assess providers before sharing personal information and disclose relevant processing arrangements to Captains.

11. Children's Information

The Captain platform is intended for individuals who meet the applicable legal age and driving eligibility requirements. It is not designed to collect applications from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when platform functionality, data practices or legal requirements change. Material updates should be communicated to affected Captains, and additional consent should be obtained where required by law.

13. Privacy Contact

For privacy questions, correction requests or concerns about personal information, contact the official ShuttleConnect privacy contact once those details are published.

A verified privacy email address and grievance contact process must be established before the platform launches.
