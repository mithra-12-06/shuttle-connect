CAPTAIN NETWORK

Captain Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before applying to become a ShuttleConnect Captain.

Important notice: This document is a draft policy for the ShuttleConnect pilot. It is not a legally reviewed agreement. The final version must be reviewed and approved by qualified legal counsel before commercial launch.

1. Eligibility and Registration

By applying to become a ShuttleConnect Captain, you confirm that you are legally eligible to operate the vehicle category for which you apply.

  • You must provide accurate personal and contact information.
  • You must hold a valid driving licence applicable to your vehicle.
  • Your vehicle must have valid registration, insurance and other legally required documents.
  • You must provide requested information for identity and vehicle verification.
  • You must promptly notify the platform if any submitted document expires, is suspended or becomes invalid.

2. Application Review and Account Activation

Submitting an application does not automatically make you an active Captain.

  • Applications are subject to administrative review and document verification.
  • ShuttleConnect may request additional information or clarification.
  • Only approved and activated Captains may access ride-acceptance functionality.
  • Following approval, an account activation link may be sent to your registered email address.
  • You must create your own password using the provided secure activation process.
  • ShuttleConnect may reject or suspend an application where eligibility or verification requirements are not met.

3. Platform Commission and Captain Earnings

The proposed pilot revenue-sharing arrangement is:

  • Captain share: 85% of the eligible ride fare.
  • Platform commission: 15% of the eligible ride fare.

The final calculation will use the eligible fare defined in the applicable fare policy. Applicable taxes, refunds, adjustments and separately disclosed third-party charges may be treated separately.

The estimated Captain earnings should be displayed before you accept a ride. The platform will maintain records of eligible fares, commissions and settlements.

The 85% / 15% arrangement is a proposed pilot policy. Its final tax treatment, deductions, settlement conditions and contractual wording must be confirmed before launch.

4. Ride Requests and Acceptance

  • You may receive ride requests based on your availability, vehicle category and service area.
  • You may accept or decline requests offered to you.
  • You should review the displayed pickup, destination, fare estimate and expected earnings before accepting.
  • A ride is assigned to you only after the platform confirms your acceptance or confirms an acknowledged administrative assignment.
  • You must not accept a ride that you cannot reasonably complete.
  • You must update your availability accurately.

5. Administrative Ride Assignments

The platform may allow administrators to assign unfulfilled ride requests to available Captains.

  • An administrative assignment must be communicated through the platform.
  • You must acknowledge an assignment before it is treated as accepted.
  • If you are unavailable, you should decline promptly so another Captain can be assigned.
  • You must not falsely report yourself as available.

6. Fare Transparency and Additional Charges

  • The fare displayed by the platform must be followed for the confirmed ride.
  • You must not demand unauthorized additional money from passengers.
  • You must not independently change the confirmed fare.
  • Any permitted tolls, parking charges or other additional costs must follow the platform's disclosed policy.
  • Changes to the route or fare must follow the platform's approval and passenger-consent process.
  • You must not misrepresent distance, ride completion or charges.

7. Payments and Settlement

ShuttleConnect may support online and cash payment methods, subject to the payment options made available for a particular ride.

  • For online payments, settlements are subject to successful payment verification and payment-provider processing.
  • For cash rides, the Captain receives the passenger's cash payment directly.
  • Cash rides may create a platform commission liability that must be reconciled according to the agreed settlement process.
  • A payout request does not necessarily mean that a bank transfer has been completed.
  • You must not falsely report a payment or settlement as completed.
  • Refunds, payment disputes and adjustments will be handled according to the applicable platform policy.

8. Cancellation and Ride Completion

  • You should avoid cancelling an accepted ride without a valid reason.
  • If an emergency or unavoidable circumstance prevents completion, notify the platform promptly.
  • You must not mark a ride as completed before the service has actually been completed.
  • Cancellation charges, if applicable, must follow the published cancellation policy.
  • Repeated misuse of cancellation or ride-completion controls may result in review.

9. Safety and Professional Conduct

  • Follow applicable traffic laws and road-safety requirements.
  • Never operate a vehicle under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances.
  • Maintain your vehicle in a safe and roadworthy condition.
  • Treat passengers, administrators and other road users respectfully.
  • Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats or abusive behaviour.
  • Do not use a mobile phone in a manner that compromises safe driving.
  • Report accidents, safety incidents and emergencies through the appropriate channels.

10. Passenger Privacy and Information

  • Use passenger information only for fulfilling the assigned ride and legitimate platform-related purposes.
  • Do not disclose passenger contact details, addresses or ride information to unauthorized persons.
  • Do not retain, copy or misuse passenger information after it is no longer needed for the permitted purpose.
  • Do not contact passengers for unrelated personal or commercial purposes.
  • Follow the platform's Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

11. Account Security

  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.
  • Do not share your account or allow another person to operate through it.
  • Notify ShuttleConnect if you suspect unauthorized account access.
  • Use the official password-reset process if you lose access to your account.
  • ShuttleConnect will never require you to disclose your existing password by email.

12. Complaints, Verification and Investigations

ShuttleConnect may review complaints, safety incidents, payment disputes and suspected misuse of the platform.

  • You may be asked to provide relevant information about a ride or incident.
  • You must cooperate honestly with reasonable verification and investigation requests.
  • False documents, fraudulent activity or deliberate manipulation of ride records may lead to account restrictions.
  • Any review or action should follow the applicable platform process and relevant law.

13. Suspension and Account Deactivation

An account may be temporarily restricted, suspended or deactivated where there are reasonable grounds, including:

  • Expired, invalid or fraudulent documents.
  • Safety violations or serious misconduct.
  • Fraudulent payment or ride activity.
  • Repeated unauthorized charges or misuse of passenger information.
  • Material violations of these terms or applicable law.

Where appropriate, the Captain should be informed of the reason and provided a reasonable opportunity to respond, subject to safety, legal and investigation requirements.

14. Changes to These Terms

ShuttleConnect may update these terms when its services, policies or legal requirements change. Material changes should be communicated to affected Captains. Continued participation after an effective update will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the required consent process.

15. Governing Law and Disputes

The final Captain agreement must specify the applicable governing law, dispute-resolution process and competent jurisdiction in India. These details must be reviewed and finalized by qualified legal counsel before the platform is launched.

16. Contact and Support

For questions about these terms, account verification, ride assignments or earnings, contact the official ShuttleConnect support channel made available to registered Captains.

Official support contact details will be published when the platform's support process is established.
